      Hollowmere

      A walled market town at the edge of the world

      You arrive with an axe and an empty purse. Sell to townsfolk who show what they want above their heads; they pay best for goods from the far, dangerous lands. Beyond the gate lie seven more lands, and the world lives on without you: settlers build towns by rich deposits, bandits move into ruins, the blight spreads, and somewhere a wizard schemes. Build outposts anywhere, hire workers and soldiers, and haul your goods home by wagon.
