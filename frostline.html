v0 scope: one level with free movement and a shovel that carves through snow at any angle, carrying, fuel, cold, two storms that drift snow back into your paths, and survivors who thaw into workers. Workers take one of three jobs (hauler, stoker, digger) or stay idle, walk only on ground that has been dug, and each has a hidden nerve that decides how cold they let themselves get. Stars between levels, tools and ice are not in this build.
